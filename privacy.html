Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and handle your personal data with care whenever you use iConnect Pre-Owned.

Information we collect

We collect the details you provide when you contact us, request a quote, make a booking, or place an order. This may include your name, email, phone number, device details, and any message content.

How we use your data

We use personal information to respond to inquiries, process bookings and orders, deliver services, and improve our website experience. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Section 18 disclosures

The responsible party for personal information processed through this site is iConnect Pre-Owned at 123 Main Street, Johannesburg, South Africa.

Providing your personal data is voluntary, but if you choose not to provide mandatory contact or booking information we may be unable to respond to your request, complete a booking, or fulfil a service.

Your information may be shared with service providers who support order processing, repair coordination, and customer communications. Personal data is not transferred outside South Africa except where required by these service providers and only on the basis of appropriate safeguards.

If you believe your data rights have been breached, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator in South Africa.

Cookies and tracking

Our site may use cookies and browser storage for basic functionality, such as keeping navigation state and enhancing the shopping experience. These tools do not collect personal content without your consent.

Your rights

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information. We will respond in line with applicable South African privacy requirements.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please get in touch via our Contact page or the support details listed on the site.
